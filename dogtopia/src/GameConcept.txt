PupPal
A Dragon City-Style Dog Habitat, Collection & Breeding Game
Web Application Concept Document
Collect, raise, and breed dogs. Each dog lives in a themed habitat island. Care for the dogs
through tasks, earn rewards, and spend them in the store to expand your park.
Core Loop
• Buy a habitat and place dogs in it.
• Do habitat tasks (feed, bathe, play, and so on).
• Earn rewards (coins, XP, gems).
• Spend rewards in the store (food, decorations, habitats, breeding).
• Level up dogs (Newborn, Puppy, Teen, Adult), then breed adults to discover rarer dogs.
Habitats
Each habitat suits certain breeds. A dog in the wrong habitat has lower comfort and earnings, so
players want a variety of islands.
Habitat Best for Example breeds
Desert Oasis Warm-climate dogs Pharaoh Hound, Basenji, Greyhound
Snowy Peak Thick-coated cold dogs Husky, Malamute, Samoyed
Forest Meadow Active all-rounders Golden Retriever, Beagle
Beach Cove Water-loving dogs Labrador, Newfoundland
Garden Park Small and toy breeds Pug, Pomeranian
Legendary Realm Epic and Legendary dogs only Cerberus Pup, Galaxy Dog
Habitat Spotlight: Desert Oasis
Suited for: dogs that handle warm climates well, with short or thin coats, lean builds, and good heat
adaptability.

• Golden sand dunes around the edges
• Sandy pathways connecting each area
• Sandstone rock formations and arches

Habitat Tasks
Dogs placed in a habitat generate tasks over time. Tapping a dog opens its current need.
Task What you do Reward
Feed Drag food to the dog's bowl XP + coins
Bath Scrub the dog at the oasis pond Coins + cleanliness bonus
Play Throw a ball or bone Happiness + coins
Refill water Tap the water station Small coins
Rest Send the dog to a shelter Restores energy
Clean habitat Clear dirt or poop Coins
Groom Brush the coat Gems (rare)
Desert Oasis specials
• Shade Time: the dog needs shade at midday or it gets tired.
• Oasis Splash: dogs can swim and cool off for a bonus.
• Treasure Dig: dogs dig up hidden items in the dunes.
• Daily and weekly quests sit on top, such as "Feed 5 dogs" or "Bathe 3 dogs in Desert Oasis".

Rewards & Store
Currencies
• Coins: earned from tasks, used for everyday purchases
• Gems: rare, earned from quests and events, used for premium items
• XP: levels up dogs and the player profile
Store categories
• Food: basic kibble, premium treats, special meals with extra XP
• Habitats: unlock new islands
• Decorations: palm trees, fountains, shelters, themed items
• Eggs and dogs: basic eggs, or special ones with gems
• Boosters: speed up breeding and growth
• Cosmetics: collars, hats, and skins
• Habitat expansion: more space for more dogs
Feeding and Evolution
Feed to gain XP. At milestone levels the dog plays an evolution animation and changes its look.
Stage Level Notes
Newborn 1-4 Needs lots of care
Puppy 5-14 Playful
Teen 15-29 Earns more coins
Adult 30+ Can breed, highest coin income
Breeding
• Two adult dogs go into the Breeding Den.
• A timer runs (1 to 12 hours), then an egg hatches.
• Offspring can come from either parent, or be a surprise hybrid.
• Dogs from the same habitat type have better odds of rare offspring.
• Special combos create secret breeds (for example, Husky + Shiba = Snow Fox Shiba).
• Players discover results through experimenting, with hints but no exact odds.

Online (Web Only) Features
• Accounts and cloud saves, so you can play on any device
• Visit friends' habitats and help with their tasks for bonus rewards
• Marketplace for trading dogs
• Leaderboards and weekly Dog Show events
• Collection Book of every dog discovered
Tech Notes
• Runs in the browser and is mobile-responsive
• Frontend: React with Phaser.js or PixiJS for the isometric view
• Backend: Node.js with PostgreSQL or Firebase
• Timers and income are calculated server-side, so they keep running while players are offline
